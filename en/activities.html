Activities

  1. Seminar on the History and Philosophy of Mathematics and Sciences (in greek)
  2. Teacher training in mathematics didactics using the history of mathematics and learning technologies.
  3. Design of teaching activities using Dynamic Geometry software.
  4. Development of collaborative learning scenario design environments.
  5. Design and implementation of microworlds to support formal mathematical thinking.

The laboratory has also:

  1. supported student teaching practice with secondary schools;
  2. collaborated with local historical archives for student education;
  3. carried out studies combining mathematics education with learning technologies;
  4. organized trainer training seminars on ICT in education;
  5. developed websites and collections on history of mathematics and mathematics education;
  6. developed educational environments based on modern learning technologies and evaluated them in schools and department courses.
